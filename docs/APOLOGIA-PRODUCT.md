# Apologia Sancta product page

The public `/products/apologia-sancta` page was refreshed from the deployed learner app and its linked Apologia Graph on 22 October 2026. This is a public beta, not a claim that every learning or research workflow is production ready.

Seven original screenshots show the rendered 3D Galaxy, its 2D view, the curated graph record, question library, expanded multiple-choice question, live-trivia room selector and research hub. Graph compatibility notices, prototype connection weights, editorial status and service-availability notices remain visible. No quiz room was joined, no player was registered, and no answer or score was submitted. Screenshots use publicly displayed content rather than invented activity.

The live bundled catalogue showed 22 topics, 265 questions and 494 cited references. Topic browsing fell back to the bundled collection. The graph rendered in compatibility mode with orbit, pan, zoom, node inspection and 2D switching. Its seeded records still require editorial review; connection weights are visualization heuristics, not truth or acceptance scores.

The learner app reported the Knowledge Engine unavailable. Its learning catalogue had no published lessons and solo practice could not load. Advanced research, official learner progress and live quiz scoring must not be advertised as verified working merely because their interfaces or source exist. Live trivia needs an available service and active room. The page preserves those limits while presenting the working catalogue and visualization tools.

No paid plan prices or subscription checkout were published in the inspected interfaces. Structured data therefore contains no price offer. Android 1.0.3 remains the latest public release; its existing APK bytes, SHA-256 digest and preview classification remain unchanged. The parent download centre remains the source of release details. A published checksum does not establish installation or signing acceptance.

Update `src/lib/apologia-content.ts`, the selected product record and screenshot captions together when availability or pricing changes. The graph source repository is private: do not publish its repository URL, administrative information or credentials. Public app links verified through the learner app are appropriate product actions.

Validation covers the actual app/graph/trivia links, absence of invented pricing, visibility of availability limits, screenshot navigation and responsive rendering.
